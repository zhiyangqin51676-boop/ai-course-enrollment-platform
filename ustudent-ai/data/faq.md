# Frequently Asked Questions — ustudent Portal

> Real-sounding student questions, with short answers. We index these alongside the handbook so the RAG agent can pick up phrasing that matches how students actually ask.

---

### Q1. How many credits do I need to graduate?

You need a minimum of 120 credits and a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0 to graduate. You also need to complete every required course in your declared major and the university-wide English Composition requirement (ENG101).

### Q2. How many courses can I take in one semester?

You can take between 12 and 18 credits per semester. That usually works out to 4–6 courses, depending on the credit value of each. To go above 18 credits you need a GPA of at least 3.5 and written approval from Academic Advising.

### Q3. Can I take CS101 and MATH101 at the same time?

No. CS101 meets Mon/Wed/Fri 09:00–10:30 and MATH101 meets Mon/Wed/Fri 09:30–11:00, so they overlap. The system will not let you enrol in both. Take one this semester and the other next.

### Q4. Can a first-year student take CS201?

Generally no. CS201 requires sophomore standing or higher and at least 30 completed credit hours, in addition to passing CS101. Most first-year students do not meet the credit-hour requirement until the end of their first year.

### Q5. How do I drop a course?

Log in to the ustudent portal, go to "My Courses", click the course you want to drop, and click the "Drop" button. The system confirms with a popup. If you drop before the end of Week 2 you get a full refund. After that, see the refund policy in §9 of the handbook.

### Q6. What is the difference between dropping and withdrawing?

Dropping happens in Weeks 1–4 and removes the course from your transcript entirely. Withdrawing happens in Weeks 5–8 and leaves a "W" on your transcript that does not affect your GPA but does show that you took and exited the course. After Week 8, neither is permitted.

### Q7. How is my GPA calculated?

GPA is the credit-weighted average of your grade points. For each course, multiply the grade points (A = 4.0, B = 3.0, C = 2.0, D = 1.0, F = 0.0) by the course's credit value, sum these, and divide by the total credits of graded courses. Withdrawn (W) and incomplete (I) courses are excluded.

### Q8. I got a D in MATH101. Can I retake it?

Yes. You can repeat any course in which you earned a D or F, once. The new grade replaces the old grade in your GPA, but both attempts stay on your transcript. To repeat a course more than once you need permission from the Dean.

### Q9. I missed the add/drop deadline. What can I do?

Email the Academic Advising Office (advising@uplus.edu) within five working days of the deadline and explain the circumstances. Late add/drop requests are granted only for documented medical or personal emergencies.

### Q10. The course I want is full. What happens if I try to enrol?

If a course is at its student cap, your enrolment attempt is placed on the waitlist. If a seat becomes available, the next student on the waitlist is enrolled automatically and notified by email. Keep checking your email during enrolment week.

### Q11. How do I check my prerequisites?

Open any course page in the portal. The "Prerequisites" section lists the required courses and any additional rules (such as minimum GPA or credit hours). If you try to enrol without meeting them, the system shows you exactly which requirement you are missing.

### Q12. Can I change my major?

Yes, once per academic year. Submit a change-of-major form to the Academic Advising Office. Courses you have already taken count toward the new major if they appear in its required or elective list.

### Q13. Where can I see my class schedule for the week?

Go to "My Schedule" in the portal. It shows your enrolled courses in a weekly grid view, with the meeting days and times of each.

### Q14. The portal says my login is wrong but I'm sure it's right.

Email support@uplus.edu with a screenshot of the error and your student ID number. Do not include your password in the email. IT Support usually replies within one working day.

### Q15. Does the portal store my password in plain text?

No. The portal stores only a hashed and salted version of your password and never logs the plaintext. If you forget your password, use the "Reset password" link on the login page; the portal will email you a one-time reset link.
